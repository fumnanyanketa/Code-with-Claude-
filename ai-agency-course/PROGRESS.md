# My progress: The AI Agency Blueprint

**North-star project:** Build your own AI-powered local-business marketing agency and take it
to your **first paying, results-getting client** — then to a repeatable $10k/month. Every
lesson's capstone builds one real component (offer, funnel, ad account, outreach system, sales
framework, fulfillment SOP, retention system).

**Daily ritual:** read the lesson → build the capstone → commit/log what you built and what
broke → tick the box. One lesson a day, ~1–2 hours.

**A grounding note before you start.** This course teaches a real skill set, but it's built from
a high-energy sales pitch. Results shown in the source (e.g. "$30k/month") are *not typical* and
not guaranteed. Cold outreach, payment handling, contracts, hiring, and ad claims all carry legal
and ethical responsibilities — each lesson flags them. Treat every income figure as illustrative,
sell only things people genuinely benefit from, and get compliance right in your own jurisdiction.
