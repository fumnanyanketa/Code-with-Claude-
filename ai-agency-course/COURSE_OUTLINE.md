# Course outline — The AI Agency Blueprint

**Source:** Owen Rensland's ~20-hour "AI Agency / SMMA Full Course" (YouTube, 2026).
**North-star project:** Build your own AI-powered local-business marketing agency and take
it all the way to your **first paying, results-getting client** — then to a repeatable
$10k/month. Every lesson's capstone builds one real component of that agency (your offer,
your funnel, your ad account, your outreach system, your sales framework, your fulfillment
SOP, your retention system), so by the end you have an operating business, not notes.

**Why the order changed.** The source video front-loads 3+ hours of Meta ad mechanics before
you've picked a niche, built an offer, or understood the fundamentals those ads depend on.
That's presentation order, not learning order. This course re-sequences the material so every
lesson depends only on earlier ones: decide *what* you sell and *to whom*, learn the operator's
lens, build the machine, master the traffic skill, get appointments, sell, deliver, and retain —
in the order you'll actually do them.

The learner is assumed to be a **total beginner**: no agency experience, no ad experience, can
use a computer. Module 0 is an optional on-ramp for accounts/tools; skip it if you already have them.

## Module 0 — Pre-flight: get your tools ready *(optional on-ramp)*
*Objective: create every account and install every tool the course uses, and confirm they work — so Lesson 1 onward is spent learning, not fighting setup.*

0. **Set up your accounts & tools** — Self-guided
   *Skill gained:* have GoHighLevel (trial), a domain on Cloudflare, Google Workspace email, Stripe, a Meta Business account + Facebook page, an AI assistant (Claude/ChatGPT), and the course spreadsheet trackers — all created and logged in.

## Module 1 — Foundations: the model & the mindset
*Objective: understand exactly what business you're building and adopt the operating mindset that separates the people who make it from the 99% who quit.*

1. **What this business actually is** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* explain, in one paragraph, the local-business marketing-agency model — who you help, the "bridge" you are, the pay-per-result offer, and why AI + now makes it work.
2. **The operator's mindset** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* set up a "signal vs noise" focus system (one mentor, 12-week blinders, implement-fast/fail-fast) so you learn from first-party data instead of drowning in content.
3. **More, Better, New — and the 5 ways people fail** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* diagnose any growth problem with the More/Better/New lever (almost always "do more"), and pre-commit to avoiding the five failure patterns (emotional ad management, ignoring data, wrong niche, overcomplicating, quitting early).

## Module 2 — Your market & your offer
*Objective: pick a niche you can dominate and build an offer so good that saying no feels irrational.*

4. **Choose your niche** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* select one micro-niche inside home improvement (or similar), kill the seven limiting beliefs about niches, and fill out an ideal-client avatar so you understand your buyer.
5. **Anatomy of a winning offer** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* assemble the six components of an offer (dream outcome, timeframe, method, safety net, pricing, qualification), pick a performance-based pricing model, and avoid the "apprentice trap."
6. **Price it to win** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* reverse-engineer your price from the client's ROI using the 5x floor, decide who pays ad spend, and word the offer around the *result* so it's perceived as a discount on dollars.

## Module 3 — The operator's toolkit: fundamentals
*Objective: install the durable mental models (systems, science, metrics) so you can rebuild any tactic when it stops working — instead of buying the next course.*

7. **Strategy, stimuli & systems** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* see any tactic as strategy + stimuli built on fundamentals, understand why systems decay and renew, and stop being a "copycat" who's helpless when a system breaks.
8. **Think like a scientist** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* run the hypothesis → test → observe → iterate loop on any channel, using a testing database, valid sample sizes (30 calls / 300 touches), and one variable at a time.
9. **Systems, metrics & the CEO dashboard** — Owen Rensland
   *Skill gained:* model your business as inputs→process→output→feedback→environment, track the KPIs that matter (led by LTGP:CAC), and find bottlenecks by reading throughput.

## Module 4 — Build your infrastructure
*Objective: stand up the software machine — CRM, funnel, video, ad account — that turns strangers into booked, shown, paying jobs.*

10. **GoHighLevel & your business foundation** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* register the business (name, domain, Cloudflare, Google Workspace, banking, Stripe), import the agency snapshot, and create your first sub-account.
11. **Configure your GHL command center** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* set up white-label, custom values, the booking calendar, Twilio/A2P phone + SMS, email deliverability, e-signable agreements, and the core automations.
12. **Build your funnel with Lovable** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* build one high-converting, mobile-first funnel (survey → booking calendar) using friction theory and ad-congruency, wired to GHL by webhook.
13. **Your VSL & Facebook page warm-up** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* record and host a video sales letter, set up your Facebook/Instagram pages, and run the $5/day engagement "warm-up" campaign that lowers your future CPMs.

## Module 5 — Meta Ads Mastery
*Objective: master the single highest-value skill in the business — turning ad spend into booked appointments — which is both how you can get clients and what you deliver to them.*

14. **Ad philosophy & your audience** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* apply More/Better/New to ads, define your niche precisely, and speak to the right one of the five awareness levels.
15. **Offer, creative & copy** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* write an ad offer (vs business offer), research winning ads in the Ads Library, and structure a hook/body/CTA and copy that stops the scroll.
16. **Make your creatives** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* produce video creatives (script, film, edit) and AI picture ads (Higgsfield + Claude), and run a proof-capturing system for social proof.
17. **Destination & tracking** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* choose funnel vs lead form by friction, integrate with GHL, and install the Meta pixel + Conversions API so Meta can learn and you can measure.
18. **Launch a campaign** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* build a full campaign in Ads Manager with 2026 targeting, run the pre-launch checklist, and launch a batch of creatives correctly.
19. **Scale with data** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* read the ad tracker against KPIs, apply kill/keep/scale thresholds and learning-phase rules, run the creative-testing cadence, and do the ad math (LTGP:CAC, client-financed acquisition, reverse budgeting).

## Module 6 — Get clients on a budget: outreach
*Objective: book sales appointments starting from $0 — with cold SMS and cold calling — because in the beginning ~90% of your job is setting appointments.*

20. **Your daily selling engine** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* run the 0→$20k/month daily workflow (sell most of the day, ~1 hour on product) and pick your acquisition channel by budget.
21. **Cold SMS Surge** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* stand up the cold-SMS system (snapshot, lead scraping, Hook/Pitch/Follow-up script) and run it live to book appointments with no ad budget.
22. **Cold Calling Catalyst** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* build the cold-calling mindset, tonality, frame and urgency, set up the power dialer, run reverse-engineered daily dial goals, and know when to remove yourself.

## Module 7 — Sales I: fundamentals & setting
*Objective: learn why people buy, then master "setting" — booking appointments that actually show up.*

23. **Sales fundamentals** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* sell scriptless using the gap/bridge, the four parts of communication (words are 7%), presence & truth, the three levels of conversation, state management, and identity-based selling.
24. **Setting that shows: identity & communication** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* adopt the setter identity and use tonality, frames/labels/mirrors, open and double-commitment questions, pattern breaks and humor to book warm appointments.
25. **Setting: qualify, book & handle objections** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* qualify (and reverse-qualify), tie down the booking live on the call, handle the 22 common setting objections, and optimize pickup/booking/show rates.

## Module 8 — Sales II: closing the deal
*Objective: run a high-ticket sales call end-to-end and collect payment on the call.*

26. **Rapport & frame** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* open a call reading PACE energy types, "chameleon" your delivery, run a mini-discovery when needed, and hold your frame.
27. **Discovery — where the sale is made** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* run discovery with presence, expansion and emotional momentum, handle both the vague and the info-bomb prospect, and move them from level one to level three (identity).
28. **Pitch, close & objections** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* transition with the yes/no/maybe frame, deliver a concise three-pillar pitch, close at the action threshold, and handle the three fears + five objection types (money first).

## Module 9 — Deliver the result: fulfillment
*Objective: onboard a client and get their campaign live fast — natively — so they see results and feel looked-after.*

29. **Onboarding & the client journey** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* run the 9-step onboarding call, collect payment + signed agreement, and manage the two tracks (invisible build + visible communication) with speed-to-lead.
30. **Build & launch the client's system** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* deliver natively (one page, one pixel), set the price anchor, build the client's GHL sub-account, creatives and funnel, and launch through the pre-launch checklist.

## Module 10 — Keep clients & scale
*Objective: turn one client into a compounding business, then scale past $30k/month on purpose.*

31. **Client Retention Mastery** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* prevent churn with a proactive communication system (proof-of-work photo, the 8 pillars), manage expectations, run frictionless billing/rebuilds, and run save/reset/exit calls.
32. **Scale to $30k/month** — Owen Rensland
    *Skill gained:* confirm the five scaling conditions (20 clients @ 85%, 3:1 LTGP:CAC), increase inputs, add infrastructure, apply leverage/asymmetries and throughput, and remove yourself by hiring.
